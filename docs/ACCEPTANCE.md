# Demonstration acceptance criteria

## Position groups

Each role must have its own applicable measurements. A striker's scoring profile cannot be calculated from midfield passing alone, and defensive profiles require explicit defensive event evidence. Missing measurements mean missing support, not proof of inactivity.

Goalkeepers have a separate measurement and reference model. Recorded saves do not establish save percentage or shot-stopping quality without the necessary shot context and denominator.

## Evidence and timing

- A displayed count must be reconstructable from event IDs for the selected player and interval.
- The interval is start-exclusive and end-inclusive.
- Related rows share a sequence identifier; rates use unique recorded sequences.
- Overlapping measurements are not added as distinct events.
- Small samples retain evidence while experimental scores are hidden.

## Honest storytelling

References are invented design profiles. No score claims historical accuracy, quality ranking, or the probability of resembling a real player. Synthetic scenarios demonstrate the pipeline; they do not validate the football model. A generated narrative must use only its supplied evidence. Changes over time require an explicit comparison package.

## Remaining submission work

The app has been hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud. Live Microsoft AI verification,
review of the updated hosted interface on desktop and mobile, a final pitch and a
sub-two-minute video remain submission work. Automated local tests and UI checks
do not establish production readiness.
