Upload the two .py files inside pl_dna into the matching GitHub pl_dna folder:
career.py and historical.py. Replace the earlier versions. No app.py, Azure secret or deployment changes are needed.

This package supersedes the seven-card package. All 25 legends now have career facts, club lists and appearance-weighted card backgrounds. Both spells with one club are combined. Championship, old First Division, youth/reserve and non-English-league appearances are excluded.

Walker, Van Dijk, Rodri, Salah and Alisson use an explicit end-of-2025/26 cutoff. De Bruyne and Alexander-Arnold use their recorded end-of-2024/25 Premier League careers. 2026/27 is excluded. These cards are snapshots, not live totals.

Alisson’s clean sheets are derived as 95 through 2024/25 plus 8 in 2025/26 = 103. His title-total field remains unavailable at this cutoff, shown as a dash rather than zero. Every card includes source links and any scope notes.

The legend comparison formula and benchmark wheel are unchanged by this card update. No Azure model calls were made. Publishing is left to you.
